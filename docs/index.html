About

QuickBMI calculates your Body Mass Index from your height and weight, in either imperial (lb, ft/in) or metric (kg, cm) units, and shows which category it falls into (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese).

QuickBMI does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
