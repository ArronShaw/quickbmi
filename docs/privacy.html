Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

QuickBMI is a Body Mass Index calculator. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health data, or usage data of any kind.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The height and weight you enter are used only to calculate a result on your device and are never saved, logged, or sent anywhere.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not request access to any device data (location, contacts, health data, etc.).

If this policy changes in the future, the change will be posted on this page.
